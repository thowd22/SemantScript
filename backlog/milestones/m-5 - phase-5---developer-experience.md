## Description

SemantScript should be extremely easy to install, debug, train with and deploy. This milestone closes the gaps found on 2026-09-25 after the first end-to-end runs by a new user: unpublished packages, a hand-installed Python half, no environment check, no cost estimate or spend cap for teachers, no built-in no-LLM training path, manual seed retries, no way to explain an answer or test a handler without training, no deployment bundle, no release management and no CI.
